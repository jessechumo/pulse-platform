pulse-platform installed into namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

If ingress-nginx is installed and the kind cluster maps ports 80/443 to the
host (see k8s/kind/kind-config.yaml):

  curl localhost/health
  curl localhost/ready

Otherwise, port-forward the Service directly:

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/pulse-app 8000:80
  curl localhost:8000/health
